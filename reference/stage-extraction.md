# Stage 2 — Extraction

## What it is

Sort raw talk into a material index and a story bank, before any book structure (chapters, outline, table of contents) is imposed. Extraction answers "what did they actually say, and what's reusable" — not "what chapter does this belong in."

## Real precedent

`GabeYoga-HQ/Detox Book/source/`:
- `Gabe-Extraction.md` — the full extraction pass.
- `Gabe-Material-Index.md` — the organized map into the transcripts, with signature sections flagged, scope guardrails, and a claim-sensitivity flag for high-risk statements.
- `GABE-STORY-BANK.md`, `STORY-LIST-MASTER.md` — the reusable-story layer, indexed separately from the material index.
- `TAKE-WHAT-WORKS.md`, `Gabe-Detox-Map.md` — secondary extraction passes organizing the same raw material from different angles.

## Two parallel outputs

1. **Material index** — signature sections, quotable lines, recurring images/refrains, and a claim-sensitivity flag for anything touching high-risk territory (medical claims, legal claims) that needs a safety/honesty pass later. Real precedent flags cancer-and-fasting statements this way: not cut, but flagged for careful framing so the finished chapter never reads as a cure claim.
2. **Story bank** — discrete, reusable anecdotes, each with a one-line summary and a status (complete scene vs. pattern-only, needs-material — see `examples.md` Example 1).

## Mechanics

1. Read the transcript(s) start to finish before sorting anything — patterns emerge across a full pass that a partial read misses.
2. Pull every recurring phrase, image, or refrain into the material index, even if its home chapter isn't obvious yet.
3. Pull every discrete anecdote into the story bank with a status: complete scene, or pattern-only (needs a follow-up capture session for the concrete instance).
4. Flag anything touching high-risk claims (medical, legal, safety) for the later revision passes — don't resolve the framing here, just flag it so it isn't lost.
5. Do not build an outline in this stage, even informally. An outline built from a partial extraction pass will miss material that a full pass would have caught.

## Refusal condition

Won't extract from anything that isn't verbatim capture. A summary, a paraphrase, or a third party's account of what the author said is not valid extraction material — route back to stage 1 for a proper capture session.
