# [Author] Story Bank

Discrete, reusable anecdotes extracted from capture, indexed separately from the material index (`MATERIAL-INDEX-template.md`). A story bank entry is a candidate for a "notebook interlude" or a chapter-opening scene — something readers will remember by name.

Shape modeled on the real `GABE-STORY-BANK.md` / `STORY-LIST-MASTER.md` (`GabeYoga-HQ/Detox Book/source/`).

---

## Entry format

```
### [Story name]
One-line: [what happens, in one sentence]
Source: [transcript file + location]
Status: complete scene | pattern-only — needs a follow-up capture for the
  concrete instance (see `examples.md` Example 1 for what a pattern-only
  entry looks like and why it can't be drafted from yet)
Candidate for: [chapter / interlude / opening scene]
```

Repeat per story. A pattern-only entry stays pattern-only — and off the drafting list — until a real capture session produces the concrete scene. Don't invent the missing specifics to promote it early.
